FAQ

Things you might be wondering

Does it work without an internet connection?

Yes. Everything runs on the phone. It works in a basement bay or in airplane mode.

Is there really no server? Where is my history stored?

Your practice history and saved swings live only in your phone's storage. Delete the app and the data goes with it.

How accurate is the video analysis?

Frame rate sets a limit, so we show a ratio as "about 3:1" rather than a false decimal. We don't round to look more precise than we are.

Is this a subscription?

No. You can keep using it free, and if you want the extras it's a single one-time payment.

When is iOS coming?

Android (Google Play) ships first; iOS is under review. Leave your email and we'll tell you as soon as it's ready.
