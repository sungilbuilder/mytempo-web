Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 2026-08-20

This is an English translation provided for convenience. The Korean version is the official text, and it prevails in the event of any discrepancy. You can read it at mytempo.co.kr/privacy.

MYTEMPO (the "App") treats your personal information with care. This policy explains what information the App handles and how.

1. Information we collect

The App does not require you to create an account or sign in, and it does not transmit or collect personal information on a server. Every feature of the App runs on your own device.

There is one exception: the "Get launch updates" form on this website (mytempo.co.kr). It stores only the email address you type in yourself. This is not something the App collects — it exists so we can contact people who asked to hear about the launch.

  • What we collect: email address and the time of the request
  • Why: to send one launch announcement
  • How long we keep it: deleted without delay after the launch announcement is sent. You may ask us to delete it sooner at any time.
  • How to withdraw: email contact@mytempo.co.kr and ask us to delete it

You can use every feature of the App without signing up for this.

2. Camera roll (photo library) access

When you use "Register my swing", the App asks for access to your camera roll so you can pick a video stored on your device. The selected video is processed on the device only and is never uploaded to an external server. The App only touches the video you explicitly select; it does not scan your photo library.

3. Data storage

Tempo marking results (segment times, ratio, name) and practice history are stored only in your device's internal storage. Deleting the App deletes that data with it. As of the current version, the App offers no cloud backup and no sync between devices.

4. Sharing with third parties and processing on our behalf

The App does not share or sell any personal information to third parties.

The launch-notification email described in section 1 is stored by the processor below. It is never provided for sale or advertising.

  • Processor: Supabase, Inc.
  • Task: storing launch-notification email addresses
  • Location: Republic of Korea (Seoul) region

5. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Contact

Questions about privacy: contact@mytempo.co.kr

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised as features are added (for example accounts, cloud sync, or analytics tools). We will announce any change in the App or by updating this page.
